Why Shipfront

Warehousing

Our warehouses provide the necessary space, security, temperature-control, and FDA compliant capabilities to store your products before shipping them to customers. Inventory is tracked across every SKU so we know where it sits, how long it has been there, and the quantity on hand.

Fulfillment

We build custom workflows to pick, pack, label, and ship your products exactly how you would like. That means working with any carrier across traditional partners, as well as last-mile and same-day delivery.

Location

Situated in downtown L.A., next to the 10, 110, and 101 freeways. We inbound your products at 1933 S. Broadway and move them out through carrier and same-day integrations. Easy access to interstate freeways, courier depots, train stations, and airports.

Integrations

Shipfront is built on the Flowspace Warehouse Management System (WMS). Through the WMS, Shipfront integrates with partners across the supply chain, from eCommerce platforms to same-day delivery services. The store talks to the floor.
